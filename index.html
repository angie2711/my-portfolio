Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

Interactive dashboard answering how orders, revenue and fulfilment vary over time, territory and product category.

Power BIPower QueryStar schema
View case study
  • Data: sample sales dataset: about 1K orders and 24K order lines.
  • Method: cleaned and shaped data in Power Query, then modelled one fact table with five dimension tables (date, product, territory, ship method, status).
  • Dashboard shows: 33.93M total due, 2.93M tax and 915.97K freight; orders by year and quarter; orders against total due by territory; order status split; product categories.
  • Read-out: Canada has the most orders; the status donut splits roughly evenly between approved, in process and shipped, with cancelled and rejected orders below 10% each.

Kickstarter Dashboard (Power BI)

Overview of crowdfunding activity across categories, countries, project states and years.

Power BIPower QueryData combining
View case study
  • Data: public Kickstarter datasets (2016 and 2018 files) combined into one table.
  • Method: trimmed text, fixed data types and merged files in Power Query before building visuals.
  • Dashboard shows: 702K projects, 73M backers, 34bn total goal and 6bn pledged; projects by main category, state and year; share of pledges by country.
  • Read-out: Film & Video (121K) and Music (99K) are the largest categories, and the US accounts for most of the pledged amount.

Movie Industry Analysis (Python)

What do budget and production company tell us about movie revenue?

PythonPandasMatplotlibSeaborn
View case study
  • Method: merged several source tables, removed irrelevant columns, handled missing values in budget, revenue, runtime, popularity and ratings, and extracted release years.
  • Findings: revenue is strongly right-skewed, with most films earning little and a few earning very much. Budget and revenue are positively related, but not consistently: budget alone does not guarantee success.
  • Companies: success rates differ widely between production companies.

ITI Statistics: Placement Report

Re-examining a placement report: are the comparisons between tracks actually fair?

PythonPandasStatistics
View case study
  • Scores: used the coefficient of variation and Z-scores to compare tracks fairly instead of raw means.
  • Salaries: mean exceeded median in every track, so salary is right-skewed and the median is the better summary.
  • Offers: binomial model: 6 interviews give about 87% chance of at least one offer. The model assumes a constant probability, which real candidates do not share.
  • Tickets and labs: Poisson fit checked through mean versus variance; box plots and fences found three outliers (38.5, 41.0, 44.7 min) in Alexandria.
  • Survey bias: reviewed a student poll where Cairo made up 38.8% of responses and 20.4% had no laptop, and designed a stratified alternative.
